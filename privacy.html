Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Unmapped is a history video channel. The Unmapped app is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

What data the app uses

When the channel owner signs in, Google gives the app permission to upload videos and to read basic details of the owner's YouTube channel. The resulting access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

Viewers

The app does not collect, store or process any data about viewers or any other people. Comments, views and other activity on the videos are handled by YouTube, TikTok and Facebook under their own privacy policies.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (YouTube) and in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions (TikTok).
